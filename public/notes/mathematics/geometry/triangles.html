Triangles: Properties, Congruence, Similarity & Centers

Triangle Inequality: The sum of the lengths of any two sides of a triangle is always strictly greater than the third side, and the difference of any two sides is strictly less than the third side.

1. Basic Angle & Side Rules

  • Sum of interior angles = 180°; sum of exterior angles = 360°.
  • Exterior Angle Theorem: An exterior angle equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles.
  • For sides a ≤ b ≤ c:
    • Acute triangle if a2 + b2 > c2
    • Right triangle if a2 + b2 = c2
    • Obtuse triangle if a2 + b2 < c2

2. Centers of a Triangle

Center Intersection Of Key Exam Property
Centroid (G) Medians Divides each median in 2 : 1 ratio from vertex
Incenter (I) Internal angle bisectors Equidistant from all 3 sides; ∠BIC = 90° + ∠A/2
Circumcenter (O) Perpendicular bisectors of sides Equidistant from all 3 vertices; ∠BOC = 2∠A
Orthocenter (H) Altitudes Lies at the right-angle vertex in a right triangle; ∠BHC = 180° - ∠A

3. Similarity & Important Theorems

  • Thales Theorem (BPT): A line parallel to one side divides the other two sides in the same ratio.
  • Area Ratio of Similar Triangles: If △ABC ~ △PQR, then Area(△ABC) / Area(△PQR) = (AB/PQ)2 = (h1/h2)2.
  • Angle Bisector Theorem: Internal bisector of ∠A meeting BC at D satisfies AB / AC = BD / DC.

Important Exam Facts

  • In an equilateral triangle of side a: Height = (√3/2)a, Area = (√3/4)a2, Inradius r = a / (2√3), Circumradius R = a / √3 (so R : r = 2 : 1).
  • For a right triangle with legs a, b and hypotenuse c: r = (a + b - c) / 2 and R = c / 2.
  • Euler Line: In any non-equilateral triangle, H, G, O are collinear and HG : GO = 2 : 1.
