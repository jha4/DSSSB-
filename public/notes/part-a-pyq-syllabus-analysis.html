4. Source quality and limitations

  • 3,253 distinct normalized question stems appear across 3,409 rows; 156 extra rows belong to exact duplicate stems across 106 duplicate groups. Repeated rows are not necessarily independent exam appearances.
  • 800 records have no Topic value. Section-level grouping is more reliable than fine-grained topic-frequency claims for the full sheet.
  • 448 records have at least one missing option field. Of these, 444 have all four separate option fields blank. The source marks 440 records as image-based; some option information is merged into the question text or depends on a figure not represented as ordinary text.
  • 101 records have no explanation text. The workbook includes 40 non-empty passage fields, so passage context matters for some comprehension questions.
  • The Paper field is blank for 764 rows and otherwise mixes year-only labels with date/shift strings. Avoid treating it as a clean year series without normalization.

5. Preparation implications

  • General Awareness is the largest section in this extract. Geography, polity and economy are its largest labelled topic groups; science, history, culture and current affairs also remain in the supplied syllabus.
  • Numerical Ability has large workbook groups in time/work, pipes/cisterns, percentage and mensuration. Do not infer that lower-count syllabus areas can be skipped.
  • English has large vocabulary groups (synonyms/antonyms, one-word substitution and idioms), but grammar and comprehension are also explicitly in the syllabus.
  • Hindi grammar and vocabulary have a strong presence; passage-based questions should be practised separately.
  • Reasoning records include series, coding-decoding, analogy, ranking and non-verbal/figure questions. Figure-based questions require the original visual, not just extracted text.
  • Teaching Methodology has 150 records in this workbook even though it is not one of the five Part A headings in the supplied syllabus excerpt. Keep it separate until the exam notification confirms its placement.

6. Interpretation

Counts describe the uploaded question workbook, not an official paper blueprint or a guarantee of future weightage. Where options, figures, passages or explanations are missing, this page does not invent them. Use the supplied syllabus as the coverage authority and the workbook as practice evidence.
