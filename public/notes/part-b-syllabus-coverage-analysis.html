2. Workbook quality and limitations

  • 1,659 distinct normalized question stems: 211 additional rows are exact repeats across 179 duplicate groups. Repeated rows remain visible with their available exam/year/shift metadata; they are not counted as independent evidence of topic importance.
  • Options: 1,391 records contain all four option texts. 479 have at least one missing option field; 362 have all four option fields blank. The website marks absent option text instead of inventing it.
  • Answer/explanation: four records have no explanation and one has no answer key. Numeric answer keys 1–4 are displayed as A–D while the original key is retained.
  • Metadata: 84 records have no year and 869 have no shift recorded. Some year cells contain full date/shift labels rather than a consistent year value.
  • The workbook mixes DSSSB TGT/PGT labels, abbreviated exam names and topic labels. It is a study compilation, not one official paper or an official topic-wise weightage document.
  • A formula-like prefix occurs in one question cell. The website neutralizes that prefix before rendering; option text is HTML-escaped and displayed as text.

3. Computer Networks and Network Security — focused audit

  • 213 records: 113 tagged Computer Networks, 69 tagged Networks & security, and 31 tagged Network Security.
  • 157 records contain all four options; 56 have one or more missing option fields. All 213 have an answer key and explanation field.
  • 26 extra rows are exact normalized repeats across 19 duplicate question groups. They are retained with source metadata.
  • Network question banks appear immediately after their related theory sections. Cards show the complete stem, available options, mapped answer and source explanation. Any missing source field is explicitly identified.

5. Revision priority — evidence versus syllabus

  • Highest practice volume: Programming/OOP, Networks/Security, Computer Fundamentals/Architecture, Digital Electronics, OS, Algorithms/Data Structures, DBMS and Web Technology.
  • Do not omit low-count syllabus areas: Teaching Methodology has only 3 records and MIS/Expert Systems only 1 in this workbook. Their syllabus theory remains examinable and is included in the website.
  • Use workbook counts to allocate extra question practice, not to predict exact future questions or skip a syllabus heading.

6. How the question bank is displayed

Each question card includes the full question stem, all option texts supplied by the workbook, mapped correct answer, source explanation and available exam/year/shift/topic metadata. Missing fields are labelled. Exact duplicate rows are retained rather than silently removed.
